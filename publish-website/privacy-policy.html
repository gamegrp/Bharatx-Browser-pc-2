Browser data on your device

The browser stores history, bookmarks, cookies, site storage, settings, saved passwords (when enabled), and download records in your local browser profile. Downloaded files are saved to the location you select. Private browsing reduces data retained in the browser after the session ends; it does not hide traffic from websites, your network, or your internet provider.

Data sent during browsing

Websites, search providers, DNS resolvers, extensions and optional browser services may receive your IP address, requested addresses, search queries, cookies and other information needed for the feature you use. Those parties operate under their own privacy policies. Suggestions, safe browsing, certificate checks, addon updates, media components and other engine services can create network traffic according to your settings and service availability.

Developer collection

This distribution disables built-in telemetry, remote experiments and upstream browser self-update checks. Nitai Studio does not operate a browsing-history collection or advertising service in this distribution. This statement does not cover data sent to websites or optional services you choose to use, or diagnostics controlled by Windows and Microsoft Store.

Optional accounts and extensions

If you sign in to an optional account, enable sync, install extensions or use another external service, its provider may process data according to its own terms and permissions. Review the settings and permissions before enabling these features.

Developer website and support

The developer website opens only when you select its link and follows that website's own policies. If you email support, we receive your email address, message and attachments. Send only information needed to resolve your request. Support correspondence is used to respond to and manage the request; you can ask us to delete it, subject to any applicable retention duties.

Your controls

Use Settings to clear browsing data, manage cookies and site permissions, disable suggestions or optional services, and remove extensions or saved passwords. To remove profile data, close the browser and remove its profile or use the applicable Windows app reset options. Export anything you want to keep first.
