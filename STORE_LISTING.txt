BharatX Browser

Short description
A desktop browser with workspaces, flexible tabs, bookmarks and privacy controls. Developed by Nitaistudio. Made in India.

Description
Browse the web with BharatX Browser by Nitai Studio. Organize tabs into workspaces, manage bookmarks and downloads, customize your browsing layout and use supported browser extensions. Built-in settings provide control over site permissions and browser data.

Find support, the Privacy Policy and Disclaimer directly inside About. The Microsoft Store edition receives updates through Microsoft Store.
